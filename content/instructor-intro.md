# Instructor introduction

This page gives general instructor information: the starting point on
your journey.


## What's it like as an instructor?

We teach online, livestream, and with *co-teaching*. That means the
lesson is a conversation between two instructors, instead of one
person alone and many students.  It's especially easy to get started
this way: co-teach with someone experienced!

You can see a demo of what livestream co-teaching is like here:
<https://youtu.be/WjmttAniZX8?t=3> (15 min, many different scenes).

Typically, teaching at workshops works like.  All of these are
discussed in the {doc}`instructor-kickstart` (a three-session prep on
top of the below):

* The instructor coordinator calls for volunteers for instructors and
  fits them into slots.  They consider people's interests, who is new
  (pairing them with experienced people), schedules, and so on.

* Co-instructors meet and plan out their lessons
  ({doc}`teaching-plan`) - basically, how they work together as
  co-instructors.  This is usually not so hard, since these have all
  been taught before.

* Review the lesson and make any necessary updates
  ({doc}`lesson-review`) - it's not supposed to be major rewrites, the
  most important part is to make sure that everything works with the
  current CodeRefinery software environments.

* Try to do one full run-through.

* Teach together!  Don't worry, you have each other.
